# Engineering Journal: Day 5 — Financial Intelligence & Recommendation Engine

## 1. Summary
Today was dedicated to refining and freezing **Milestone 8 — Financial Intelligence & Recommendation Engine**. We successfully integrated a centralized, config-driven evaluation engine that computes financial ratios, multi-period growth trends, health scores, distress risk profiles, and recommendation ratings. We also built an explainability service returning positive/negative signals and structured reasoning logs, alongside a unified dashboard API route with complete workspace tenant isolation.

## 2. Major Accomplishments
*   **Centralized Config**: Moved all scoring weights, trend parameters, and policy thresholds to `app/core/financial_config.py`.
*   **Registry-driven Ratio Calculations**: Deployed a dynamic `RatioDefinition` registry in `app/domain/rules/ratio_registry.py` validating inputs and classifying qualitative statuses (Excellent, Healthy, Watch, Weak, Critical).
*   **Multi-Period Trend engine**: Classifies growth indicators across chronological filings into accelerating, decelerating, recovery, decline, stable, or volatile states in `app/domain/rules/trend_engine.py`.
*   **Weighted Scoring Engine**: Calculates overall and category-level health scores (liquidity, profitability, leverage, efficiency, cash flow, growth) in `app/domain/rules/health_scoring.py`.
*   **Risk Distress Engine**: Automatically flags severe, moderate, and low-level risks based on solvency and capital indicators in `app/domain/rules/risk_engine.py`.
*   **Policy-driven Recommendation Engine**: Evaluates active policy thresholds against health, severe risks, and growth constraints in `app/domain/rules/recommendation_engine.py`.
*   **ORM Persistence Mappings**: Autogenerated migration `89ee9532b883` mapping all engine outputs to database tables with audit trails.
*   **Consolidated Dashboard Endpoint**: Returns health, recommendations, risks, trends, confidence breakdowns, and versions in a single payload under `GET /companies/{id}/dashboard`.
*   **Hardened RAG Grounding**: Strengthened LLM prompt safety rules in `app/prompts/answer_prompt.md`.

---

## 3. Architectural Decisions
*   **Determinism Before Intelligence**: All mathematical calculations, health scores, and investment recommendations are computed deterministically. The LLM is strictly prohibited from recalculating or overriding metrics, serving only to explain inconsistencies between filings and deterministic outputs.
*   **Decoupled Registry Pattern**: Separated ratio definitions from computation logic. New ratios can be added to the registry by declaring their formula and qualitative boundaries without editing the service.
*   **Audit Logging Persistence**: Deployed a `RecommendationHistory` entity to preserve a complete versioned log of recommendations, policies applied, and step-by-step reasoning logs.
